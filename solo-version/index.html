Talk to Elder Maren (gold “!” above her head) for quests and to Borin to trade. The fountain in town heals you. Enemies with a blue glow are champions: tougher, with better loot. Everyone near a kill gets their own loot and full experience, so group up.

After level 10, cross the ridge to the outer lands. Three more towns wait there: Frosthaven (north), Cinderwatch (east) and Mirebrook (south), each with new quests and a trader with better wares. Every town has a chest for storing items and a waystone for fast travel. You rise again in the last town you visited.
