How to play

Left click
Walk there. Click an enemy to attack it until it falls. Hold to keep walking toward the cursor.
W A S D
Walk directly.
Right click / 1
First ability, aimed at the cursor.
2 · 3 · 4
More abilities, unlocked at level 3, 5 and 8.
Shift + click
Attack toward the cursor without moving.
Q
Drink a healing potion.
R
Ride your horse, if you have one (Borin in Duskhold sells them). You run 60% faster, but fighting or getting hit throws you off.
T
Open a town portal. Stand still for a moment and it takes you to the last town you visited. Walk into the portal in town to go straight back. Friends can use your portal too.
Right click an item
Drop it on the ground (in your bags). Anyone can pick it up, so this is how you give items to a friend.
Barrels & urns
Click barrels, crates, urns, bone piles and coffins to smash them for gold, potions and items. Your attacks smash them too. Golden treasure chests hold the best loot. Some coffins are not empty…
C or I
Character and bags. Click an item to equip it.
L · M
Quest log · world map.
Enter
Chat with everyone in the realm. Click a name in the chat to block that player, or type /block name and /unblock name.
Mouse wheel
Zoom the camera.

Talk to Elder Maren (gold “!” above her head) for quests and to Borin to trade. The fountain in town heals you. Enemies with a blue glow are champions: tougher, with better loot. Everyone near a kill gets their own loot and full experience, so group up.

After level 10, cross the ridge to the outer lands. Three more towns wait there: Frosthaven (north), Cinderwatch (east) and Mirebrook (south), each with new quests and a trader with better wares. Every town has a chest for storing items and a waystone for fast travel. You rise again in the last town you visited.

Treasure goblins sometimes appear out in the wild with a sack of gold on their back. They run away and vanish after a while, so chase them down! Monsters also drop red health globes: walk over one to heal.

Six heroes: Warrior (tank), Mage (fire and frost), Ranger (bow and healing), Paladin (hammer, stuns, heals and shields the party), Necromancer (raises skeletons that fight for him) and Assassin (fast blades and shadow steps).

Dungeons: five stairways lead underground (look for ▼ on the map): the Goblin Warren in Thornwood, the Catacombs under the graveyard, the Frozen Depths, the Molten Deep and the Sunken Temple. Fight through the rooms to the lord at the bottom, who guards a treasure chest. The stairs in the first room take you back up.
